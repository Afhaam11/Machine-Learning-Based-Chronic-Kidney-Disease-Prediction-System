How an estimate is made

Your values are cleaned and encoded in one place, so the form, the batch importer and the training code can never disagree. Seven classifiers (logistic regression, decision tree, random forest, XGBoost, k-nearest neighbors, support vector machine and Gaussian naive Bayes) each estimate the probability of CKD. The ensemble averages those seven probabilities.

Missing values are filled with typical values learned from the training data, inside each model's own pipeline. The result says how many measurements it was built from and whether the models agree.

Why this result

Contributions are Shapley values: the model walks from a typical healthy profile to your values one measurement at a time, over many random orders, and shares the change in risk fairly between the measurements. Together they add up exactly to the difference between the healthy-profile risk and your estimate.

The what-if panel re-scores the same patient while one measurement is swept across its range, holding everything else fixed.

Reading the risk strip

    The large marker is the ensemble. Each small dot is one model, so a tight cluster means agreement and a spread means the case is close to the decision line at 50%.

    Limits to keep in mind

    • The training data is 400 patients from a single published dataset, and many values are missing. Near-perfect scores on it do not predict clinical performance.
    • Typical ranges shown on the form are general adult reference intervals. Laboratories, age and sex all shift them.
    • Reliability is a rule of thumb based on completeness, model agreement and training range. It is not a statistical confidence interval.
    • This is not a medical device and does not provide a diagnosis.

    Privacy

    The server scores what you send and keeps no record of it. Your history is saved in this browser only, and the Clear button on the History page removes it.

    Data

    The Chronic Kidney Disease dataset from the UCI Machine Learning Repository: 400 patients, 24 measurements, 250 with CKD and 150 without.
